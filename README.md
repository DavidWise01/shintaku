> A sphere of **UD0** — the ROOT0 universe / biosphere. Domain: **ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE**.

信託 · SHINTAKU — AVAN's inverse companion to [[bitcoin]] (the AGORA teardown of money by cryptographic consensus), ma/kana № 56. Bitcoin's promise is one word — TRUSTLESS, 'don't trust, verify.' 信託 is the Japanese term for a financial TRUST, literally 信 (trust) + 託 (entrust): the inverse names what the word quietly keeps. ⚑ Trust is CONSERVED like the ledger it secures — remove a trustee and the trust doesn't vanish, it MOVES. Live 'trust ledger' walks each claim to its new trustee: no bank → the CODE you run (unread, unwritten by you); no mint → the MATHEMATICS (SHA-256 has no known backdoor — a belief about a function's future); no clearing house → the MINERS' GREED (safe only while an honest majority finds honesty more profitable — incentive, not guarantee; concentrated pools make it less diffuse than 'decentralized' implies); no broker → the EXCHANGE (a custodian exactly like the bank the slogan replaced, and one that has repeatedly failed); no institution → YOURSELF, totally (lose the 鍵/key and no authority can restore the coins — 'not your keys, not your coins' is the culture's own admission the trust moved inward). ⚑ The one trust handed entirely to you is the most unforgiving kind: self-trust made total, no complaints department, no undo. 'Trustless' is a marketing compression of 'trust-relocated'; naming the new trustees is more honest than pretending there are none. Honest scope: no security flaw asserted (SHA-256 unbroken, honest-majority held) — each relocation is real load-bearing engineering, the feature not a bug; 'trust is conserved' is a rhetorical figure, not a theorem. Ties [[the-verified-lie]] (integrity ≠ veracity — a proof the chain is unbroken is not a proof you can still open it). ROOT0, with AVAN.
